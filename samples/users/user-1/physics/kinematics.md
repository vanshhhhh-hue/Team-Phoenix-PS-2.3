# Kinematics

## Motion in a straight line

Acceleration describes the rate of change of velocity. For constant acceleration, use v = u + at. Starting from rest means u = 0; displacement and velocity are different quantities.

Key relation: v = u + at

## Worked practice

v = u + at = 0 + 2 × 3 = 6 m/s.
