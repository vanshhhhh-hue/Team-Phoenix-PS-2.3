# Work, Energy & Power

## Kinetic energy

Translational kinetic energy is one half of mass times speed squared. Doubling speed quadruples kinetic energy. The work done by the net force equals the change in kinetic energy.

Key relation: K = ½mv²

## Worked practice

K = ½mv² = ½ × 2 × 3² = 9 J.
