# Ray Optics

## Converging lens

A thin converging lens forming a real image obeys 1/f = 1/u + 1/v when u and v are positive distance magnitudes. Therefore f = uv/(u+v). With Cartesian signs, the equivalent formula is 1/f = 1/v - 1/u.

Key relation: 1/f = 1/u + 1/v (positive magnitudes for real object/image)

## Worked practice

A converging lens forms a real image 20 cm from the lens of a real object 20 cm away. What is the focal length? Use positive distance magnitudes.

f = uv/(u+v) = (20 × 20)/(20+20) = 10 cm.
